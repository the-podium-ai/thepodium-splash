How it works

Driver data, analysis and recommendation.

The technology behind the platform, in four layers.

01 · Collection

Telemetry with consent

Our own collector reads the telemetry of your sim sessions, with your consent, and sends it to the platform. Nothing is collected without you knowing.

02 · Data

Progress over time

Every session, track and car goes into your history. Evolution over time, not a single lap, is what feeds the assessment and the recommendations.

03 · Analysis

Analysis and recommendation

Objective analysis of your sessions shows where you lose time. Generative AI translates it into driver language and points to the next step.

04 · Agents and platform

AI that acts along the journey

AI assistants accompany the driver along the journey, with clear boundaries on what they can and cannot do. Web platform, cloud-native, privacy by design (LGPD, GDPR and CCPA).
